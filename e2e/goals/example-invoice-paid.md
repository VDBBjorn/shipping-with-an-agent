# A session gets invoiced and paid

Sign in: owner

The owner saw milo today without an appointment in the calendar. She books that
session afterwards, marks that he came, invoices it and sends the invoice to his
mother, rosa. Rosa pays by bank transfer, the owner records the payment, and rosa
sees in the portal that the invoice is paid.

## Limits
- Start from the home screen.
- No search box, no assistant.

## Pass
- Rosa received the invoice mail.
- The invoice from this run is fully paid.
- Rosa sees that invoice as paid in the portal.
