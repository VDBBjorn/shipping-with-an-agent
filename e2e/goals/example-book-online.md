# A new client books a first appointment online

Sign in: none
Start at: /book

Someone who has never been to the practice opens its public booking page. They
pick the first free time this week, fill in their name and email address, and
send the request. They confirm it with the link in the mail they get. The owner
then sees the appointment in her calendar.

## Limits
- Use only what the booking page offers. No account, no sign-in.
- Fill in the form at the speed of a person. The page refuses a form that is sent within a few seconds.
- Confirm through the link in the mail, never by opening the confirmation address directly.

## Pass
- The visitor received a mail with a confirmation link.
- After the link is followed, the page says the appointment is confirmed.
- The appointment from this run is in the owner's calendar, at the time the visitor picked.
