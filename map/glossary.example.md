# Glossary (example)

This is the entry format, with three terms from the invented example app. Replace the terms with your own and keep the format.

## The format

```markdown
**<Term>**:
<What it is, in one or two sentences. What it is not, if people mix it up.>
_Avoid_: <the words not to use for it, each with a short reason where that helps>
```

Rules for an entry:

- One term per concept. The term is the word used in class names, routes, test names and on screen.
- The `_Avoid_` line lists the synonyms an agent or a colleague would reach for. Say where a word on that line is a term of its own.
- Terms are in English, like the code. The words a user sees in another language live in the translations, not here.
- When a term is used on screen under another label, add a line `_On screen_: <label>`.

## Terms

**Appointment**:
A client's booked time: a start and a duration. It says nothing about whether the appointment actually took place.
_Avoid_: session (that's what actually happened), booking, consultation

**Session**:
What actually happened at an appointment: the client came, and the work was done. A session is what gets invoiced. An appointment the client missed has no session.
_Avoid_: appointment (that's the booked time), visit, treatment

**Contact**:
A person who may act for a client: a parent or a partner. A contact signs in to the portal, receives the client's invoices and can book for the client. A contact is not a client and has no appointments of their own.
_Avoid_: guardian, relative, user (that's anyone who can sign in), customer
